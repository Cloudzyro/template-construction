# Unslop pass

Done with the unslop skill (by Damir Sokolovsky, @heydamir, CC BY 4.0), 25 November 2026.

## Audit

Slop level: light. Built against the signs from the start; the first render showed composition problems rather than slop.

- Hero headline ran to five lines and pushed the stats off the first screen; the framing drawing sat small beside it
- Dimension labels in the hero drawing were too small to read
- Kitchen "after" illustration: a pendant cord ran down the middle of the window
- Inner pages: a large dead band between the dark page header and the first content
- No uppercase tracked labels, pill badges, glows, gradient text, emoji, italic emphasis, hype words, looping motion or 700+ weights found on any page

## Direction

- Product: the site of a residential builder, so homeowners can see the work, trust the license and ask for a quote
- Character: plain-spoken, sturdy, exact
- World: a framing plan at the site trailer, steel tape, lumber stamps, safety yellow on a dark jobsite morning
- Moments: the hairline house frame in the hero with one yellow header beam; the before and after slider on each project
- Signature: architectural dimension lines (tick ends and a measurement) under section headings only

## What changed

- Copy: headline shortened to "Remodels and additions, built by our own crew"; the kinds of work moved into the subhead
- Layout: the hero gives the drawing more width and crops its viewBox so the frame reads at a glance; stats now sit on the first screen
- Visuals: dimension labels in the drawing enlarged; kitchen pendants moved off the window
- Spacing: a page header followed by a section now uses 40 to 64px, not the full section padding
- Checked at 360px on every page: no horizontal scroll
